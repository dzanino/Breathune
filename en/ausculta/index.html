What Ausculta is

Ausculta (from Latin auscultare, to listen closely) is an educational tool for learning auscultation. At its core is an atlas of textbook heart, lung and bowel sound excerpts from open research databases, each with a description, source and licence.

It is an app for listening and comparing, not measuring. It does not measure, assess or recognise body sounds: you listen and you decide. It comes from the same workshop as Breathune and is meant for medical and nursing students, doctors, and anyone who wants to listen more carefully.

What is in the app

1

Sound atlas

Heart, lung and bowel sounds, plus ECG excerpts with sound on one timeline. Every excerpt has a description, source and licence.

2

Quiz

An excerpt plays and you pick the type of sound. You immediately see the textbook description. Only your answers are scored.

3

Compare two

Play one excerpt, then the other, and listen for the difference. The app remembers which excerpts you have already heard.

4

Listening practice

Sound from the iPhone microphone is amplified and played to your headphones, like Live Listen. Bell, Diaphragm and Wide filters, a waveform and a spectrum.

Free and Ausculta Pro

Listening practice, filters, the quiz, comparison, the guide, part of the atlas and all ECG excerpts with sound are free. A one-time purchase, Ausculta Pro, adds unlimited recordings, sharing of unfiltered copies, a custom filter band and the remaining atlas excerpts. A voluntary contribution to development unlocks nothing.

FAQ

Is Ausculta a medical device?

No. It is an educational tool for learning to listen. It does not measure, diagnose or replace an examination by a doctor. If you have health concerns, see a doctor.

Where do the sound excerpts come from?

From open research databases (for example PhysioNet) and one sound library. Each excerpt shows its source and licence, and a summary is in the app under About.

Does Ausculta upload my recordings?

No. Everything is processed on the phone and recordings stay on the device.

Ausculta is an educational listening tool. It is not a medical device, does not measure or diagnose, and does not replace an examination by a doctor.
