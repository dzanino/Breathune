About the app

Breathune is a guided breathing app by the Slovak physician Ján Šikuta, MUDr. It guides you with visuals, breath sound, gentle haptics and, if you like, a calm voice (the voice is created with AI).

The main program is the Šikuta Method: breathing in a deepening 4 · 2 · 6 wave. It comes with five more programs, your own custom programs, Apple Watch and Wear OS (including the one-minute Calm now) and Snore No More, an overnight snore tracker that runs entirely on the phone.

Breathune is a wellness app, not a medical device.

How to try the app

  • You can install Lite Breathune right away; it includes the Šikuta Method.
  • We provide the full version on request. Write to admin@breathune.eu with the name of your publication and whether you test on iPhone or Android. For iPhone we send an App Store download code; for Android we arrange access through Google Play.
  • Test tip: 7 days of the Šikuta Method and the one-minute Calm now on a watch. That is enough to form an opinion.
