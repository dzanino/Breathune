Breathune › What's new

The latest releases of Breathune from the App Store, the same notes you see when updating. Only released versions are listed.

Breathune 5.1.1

November 2026

  • Apple Watch: when you start Power Breath directly on the watch, you now see the same safety notice as on iPhone.

Breathune 5.1

November 2026

  • Voice guide: a calm female or male voice speaks cues like Breathe in, Hold and Breathe out during your session. Turn it on in Options or before a session. The voice is created with AI.

Breathune 5.0

November 2026

  • Live heart rate: during a session in Combo mode (iPhone + Apple Watch), your iPhone shows the heart rate from your watch and the circle gently pulses with it.
  • Calm Start: the Šikuta Method with Apple Watch begins only once your heart rate has settled for a moment (12 seconds at most). You can turn it off in Options.
  • Calm Now on Apple Watch: one minute of calm, even breathing (5 seconds in, 5 seconds out) right at the top of the list, also as a watch face complication. On newer watches you can start it with a double tap of your fingers (Double Tap).
  • Live Activity: on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and in StandBy you see the current breathing phase and the countdown, with an End button.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: say, for example, “Start Sikuta Method in Breathune”, or pick a program through Shortcuts.
  • From version 4.6: accessibility (Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, text size), more reliable night tracking (a night closes by itself after 12 hours, a phone call no longer ends it, low battery warning) and correct word forms with numbers.

Breathune 4.5

October 2026

  • Apple Watch: your heart rate during the session and the average at the end are larger and easier to read.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch now stay in the same rhythm after a phone call or switching to another app. Pausing on one device pauses the other too.
  • Sound on Bluetooth and AirPlay speakers is quieter and better balanced. After the session the background slowly fades down for rest and sleep.

Breathune 4.4

October 2026

  • New languages: Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil) in the app, on Apple Watch, in the widget and the complication.
  • Small text fixes.

Older versions (1.0 to 4.3) are in the App Store under Version History.

The Android app has its own version numbering; see What's New on Google Play.
